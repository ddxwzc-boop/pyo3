Avoid the `Once` and `Mutex` of the lazy exception state: `PyErr`'s internal state machine is now a single atomic word (progressing monotonically from lazy through normalizing to normalized, or poisoned if normalization panics) plus a thread key for re-entrancy detection. `PyErr::from_type` and `PyErr::from_value` additionally store message-like arguments (`&'static str`, `String` and pre-materialized `Py` objects) inline in the lazy state instead of boxing a closure, extending the `PyErr::new` fast path to them. This reduces the cost of constructing, dropping, restoring/fetching and lazily normalizing exceptions, especially without the GIL.
